# Rotten Tomatoes asset terms

Owner: Fandango Media / Rotten Tomatoes.
Source and usage guidelines: https://www.rottentomatoes.com/help_desk/licensing
Retrieved: 2026-10-10.

These are proprietary brand assets, not covered by the Simple Icons CC0 license.
The official page directs users to request asset access and brand guidelines via
its business proposal form. Inclusion here does not grant a trademark or
redistribution license.

Its guidelines require brand attribution and links to the corresponding title
pages when displaying scores. Fresh/Hot and Rotten/Stale identify opposite sides
of the 60% score threshold. Certified Fresh and Verified Hot badges are reserved
for titles explicitly awarded those designations. The page restricts alterations
without prior permission; the technical packaging changes are recorded in
ATTRIBUTION.md and sources.json. Consult the linked guidelines for actual use.
